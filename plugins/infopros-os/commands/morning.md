Give the morning brief. Follow the team-data skill, especially its writing rules. Today is the date you know; "last
night" is yesterday.

Focus: $ARGUMENTS

Gather, then write at most 12 lines in total:

1. **Last night's webinars:** `webinar_numbers` without a client, when "yesterday". One line each: client,
   registrants, show rate, cost per registrant, and the move against their usual.
2. **Bookings and cash posted:** `results` when "yesterday". One line per client with the numbers and links. Then
   one line with how many numbers need a teammate to confirm (review `unclear`), if any.
3. **Costs that jumped:** `ad_numbers` grouped by account, when "yesterday", compare true. Only accounts whose cost
   per registration rose by a third or more on real spend; one line each.
4. **Ad changes:** for the clients in 1 and 3 only, `ad_changes` for yesterday, kinds budget and status. One line per
   client with who changed what.
5. **Today:** `launches` when "today". One line listing today's webinars.

Use these headings in bold, and leave out a section with nothing in it. No intro, no closing line.
